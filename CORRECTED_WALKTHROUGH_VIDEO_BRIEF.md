# Corrected PromptForge Walkthrough Video Brief

## Correction approach

The first video versions used viewport-only screen frames, which did not reveal complete pages or enough of the relevant controls. The revised landscape and mobile videos use **full-page captures with slow top-to-bottom screen movement**. Each voice-over scene describes only controls visible during that scene. The revised videos are intentionally longer and have no rapid cuts.

## Narration and visual alignment

| Scene | Visible page and movement | Voice-over script |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Builder: header and category cards | “Welcome to PromptForge. This is the Builder, your starting point for turning an idea into a useful AI brief. Use these category cards to choose the kind of work you want to make.” |
| 2 | Builder: guided fields and live output | “After choosing a category, describe what you are making, your primary goal, audience, platform, tone, and any helpful details. The live prompt updates as you decide.” |
| 3 | Builder: output actions | “Review the live prompt before you use it. Copy sends it to your clipboard. Save keeps work on your account after sign-in. Refine lets you adjust the brief.” |
| 4 | Library: heading and search | “Open Library from the header to start with a proven prompt. Search by a keyword, then narrow the results with the category and access filters.” |
| 5 | Library: catalog cards and pagination | “Browse the prompt cards at your own pace. Use sorting and Load more to move through the catalog, then open a prompt to adapt its structure.” |
| 6 | Pricing: plans and unlock path | “On Pricing, compare the free shelf with full access. You can keep using free prompts, or follow the unlock path when you need the complete workshop.” |
| 7 | Sign in: account benefits and choices | “Sign in with your email, magic link, Google, or GitHub when available. Signing in keeps saved prompts and your access details connected to your account.” |
| 8 | Guides: article and navigation | “Visit the Guides for practical prompt-engineering examples. Each guide explains a method, then links you back to the Builder or Library to put it into practice.” |
| 9 | About: mission and audience | “About explains the PromptForge mission and who it is built for: creators, marketers, freelancers, developers, and teams who need clearer AI instructions.” |
| 10 | Contact and theme controls | “Use Contact to send a message or open WhatsApp. You can also choose dark, light, or system appearance from the theme control, and navigate easily from the mobile menu.” |
| 11 | Builder: closing summary | “That is the PromptForge workflow: build a brief, browse templates, learn the method, and get help when you need it.” |

## Pacing standard

Each scene should stay on screen through its full matching narration, plus two seconds of reading time. The page motion must remain slow enough to see all major visible sections and controls.
