# PromptForge Mobile Walkthrough Video Brief

## Format and pacing

This is a **9:16 portrait mobile walkthrough** for new users. It uses a deliberately paced screen tour: each major scene remains visible for **at least eight seconds** so viewers can read the page, find the key controls, and understand the next step. Captions appear in a persistent lower-third and stay on screen for the complete scene.

## Scene sequence

| Scene | Page | Minimum dwell time | Guidance |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Builder | 9 seconds | Start from the Builder and choose a category. |
| 2 | Builder | 9 seconds | Complete the guided brief and watch the live output take shape. |
| 3 | Library | 9 seconds | Search the prompt shelf, use filters, and browse prompt work orders. |
| 4 | Pricing | 8 seconds | Explain free access and the optional lifetime unlock path. |
| 5 | Sign in | 8 seconds | Explain email sign-in, magic-link access, and saved work. |
| 6 | Guides | 8 seconds | Show learning resources for clearer prompts and better workflows. |
| 7 | About | 8 seconds | Show the mission and who PromptForge is built for. |
| 8 | Contact | 9 seconds | Show the contact form and direct WhatsApp route. |
| 9 | Builder | 9 seconds | Close with the header navigation and the next prompt-building step. |

## Accuracy constraints

Use real mobile screens and visible controls only. Do not show personal account data, redemption codes, admin settings, a purchase completion, or invented user feedback.
