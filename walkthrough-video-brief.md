# PromptForge New-User Walkthrough Video Brief

## Purpose
Create a concise onboarding video for first-time PromptForge users. The video should explain the product journey clearly and make the Builder feel approachable for beginners.

## Audience
New creators, marketers, freelancers, developers, educators, and small-business users who want to create better AI prompts without studying prompt theory.

## Target format
A 60–75 second landscape product walkthrough in the Workshop Noir aesthetic: black and charcoal surfaces, ember-orange accents, steel-gray details, clean editorial typography, subtle workshop/forge atmosphere, and restrained motion.

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene | On-screen message | Voiceover intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–7s | Brand intro with PromptForge mark and dark forge texture | “Forge prompts that ship.” | Welcome the user and frame PromptForge as a practical prompt workshop. |
| 7–20s | Builder overview with category cards | “1. Choose what you’re making” | Explain that beginners can pick a category such as Social Media, Writing, Marketing, Code, Image, Video, Business, or Customer Service. |
| 20–34s | Builder controls and live work order preview | “2. Add a few details” | Show project type, audience, platform/format, tone, and primary goal; emphasize that no prompt theory is required. |
| 34–46s | Live preview assembles role, task, audience, format, tone, goal, deliverables, and constraints | “3. Watch your prompt take shape” | Explain that the structured preview updates as choices change and stays populated for every category. |
| 46–58s | Action row: Copy, Save, Refine, Reset | “4. Use it immediately” | Show copying the prompt, refining it, resetting it, and signing in to save work. |
| 58–68s | Library search and filters | “Browse 3,000 ready-to-use prompts” | Explain the searchable catalog, category filters, and FREE/LOCKED access states. |
| 68–75s | Unlock/account CTA and closing brand frame | “Start building in PromptForge” | Invite the viewer to try Builder first, then create an account or explore lifetime access when ready. |

## Visual constraints
Use authentic-looking product UI compositions with large readable labels, no invented testimonials, no fake ratings, and no claims beyond the product’s actual functions. Keep transitions smooth and short, with ember-orange highlights guiding attention. Avoid tiny illegible interface text, busy backgrounds behind instructional copy, and generic SaaS blue/purple styling.

## Delivery
Preferred output: MP4, 16:9, suitable for a landing-page onboarding section or first-login welcome modal. Include captions or readable on-screen labels so the video remains understandable without audio.
