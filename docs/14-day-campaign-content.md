# PromptForge 14-Day Social Media Campaign Content

## Campaign name

**From Blank Page to Forged Prompt: A 14-Day PromptForge Workshop**

## How to use this schedule

Publish one core story each day and adapt the same idea to the language of each platform. LinkedIn should carry the most context, Facebook should feel conversational and shareable, Instagram should lead visually, TikTok should move quickly through a demonstration, and X should condense the insight into a sharp observation or short thread. Use the 16:9 screenshots for LinkedIn, Facebook, and X. Use the 9:16 screenshots for TikTok and Instagram Reels. Each day has one primary CTA; do not give the audience several competing actions.

# Platform publishing notes

LinkedIn posts should generally be published during professional browsing windows, with the exact time adapted to the account’s analytics. Facebook should prioritize community-friendly copy and shareable demonstrations. Instagram should use the strongest screenshot or a short Reel cover, with the first line functioning as the hook. TikTok should show the product interaction immediately and avoid spending the first seconds on a logo animation. X should publish the observation first, then add the product link or screenshot in a follow-up reply if the post needs more context.

The campaign should be tested with two hook styles: a question-led hook such as “What if your next AI result started with a better brief?” and a demonstration-led hook such as “Watch this rough idea become a structured prompt.” Keep the visual, offer, and CTA constant while changing one hook at a time. Retain the version that produces stronger saves, shares, qualified website clicks, and WhatsApp conversations.
