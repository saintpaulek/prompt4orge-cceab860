# PromptForge 14-Day Social Campaign Framework

## Campaign objective

The campaign will introduce PromptForge as a practical AI prompt-building workspace for creators, marketers, freelancers, business owners, educators, and teams. Its primary conversion objective is to move viewers from awareness to a first Builder session, then toward account creation, Library exploration, or lifetime access through the main WhatsApp Business conversation.

## Core campaign idea

**From blank page to usable prompt.** Each day will show one recognizable work moment, demonstrate how PromptForge turns a rough intention into a structured prompt, and finish with one low-friction next step. The campaign should feel like a guided workshop rather than a series of product announcements.

## Story-led content arc

The fourteen days follow a five-part sequence. Days 1–3 establish the product and the audience problem without overemphasizing pain points. Days 4–6 demonstrate the Builder experience from an empty state to a complete forged prompt. Days 7–9 show practical breadth through the Library, copying, saving, and reuse workflows. Days 10–11 build trust through clarity about access, contact, and responsible use. Days 12–13 turn the demonstrations into creator-focused use cases and short tutorials. Day 14 closes with a direct invitation to try PromptForge and share it with someone who creates content or runs a business.

## Audience segments

The primary audience is Nigerian and international English-speaking creators, social media managers, freelancers, virtual assistants, small-business owners, marketers, educators, and early-stage teams. Messaging should use clear language, practical examples, and familiar work scenarios such as content calendars, product launches, customer replies, short-form video ideas, SEO briefs, and business planning.

## Platform roles

| Platform | Primary role | Recommended content behavior |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Authority, professional education, and B2B discovery | Explain the workflow, show a before-and-after, and invite thoughtful comments or trials. |
| Facebook | Community reach and practical sharing | Use accessible storytelling, concise demonstrations, and shareable educational posts. |
| Instagram | Visual product proof and creator discovery | Lead with a strong visual hook, carousel, Reel, or screen recording with concise captions. |
| TikTok | Fast discovery and demonstration | Use a spoken hook in the first two seconds, visible interaction, captions, and one clear CTA. |
| X | Conversation, commentary, and product-led discovery | Use concise observations, short threads, screenshots, and direct replies to relevant conversations. |

## Content framework

Every daily post will use the **HOOK → SCENE → TRANSFORMATION → PROOF → CTA** framework. The hook names an aspiration or familiar work moment. The scene shows the relevant PromptForge screen or workflow. The transformation explains what changed. The proof points to a visible feature rather than an unsupported performance claim. The CTA asks the audience to perform one action only, such as trying the Builder, opening the Library, saving a prompt, or sending a WhatsApp message.

## Conversion path

The default path is social post → PromptForge homepage or Builder → first prompt → sign-up or Library exploration → lifetime-access conversation through the main WhatsApp Business chat. Paid posts should use one destination per ad set and preserve source-specific pre-filled WhatsApp messages where the CTA is an Unlock action.

## Claim and compliance rules

The campaign should describe visible product capabilities and avoid promising guaranteed business growth, income, rankings, sales, or creative results. The posts should not fabricate testimonials, customer reviews, ratings, or performance numbers. When a creator is paid, gifted access, or receives another benefit, the post should use clear disclosure such as “Paid partnership with PromptForge” or “Ad.”

## Measurement plan

Track reach, three-second video views, average watch time, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, Builder starts, sign-ups, Library actions, WhatsApp conversations, and unlock inquiries. Compare creative by hook, platform, audience segment, and CTA. The first optimization decision should prioritize saves, shares, and qualified clicks rather than impressions alone.

## Visual system

The visual language will retain PromptForge’s Workshop Noir aesthetic: charcoal and near-black surfaces, ember-orange accents, steel-gray dividers, clear white typography, and close-up UI framing. The supplied 16:9 and 9:16 screenshots will be used as product proof. Where a daily theme needs two extra days beyond the ten screenshot states, the visual direction will use a crop, zoom, carousel sequence, or a repeated screenshot with a different highlighted region rather than inventing a new product screen.

## Daily structure

Each day in the final Word document will include the date slot, campaign objective, audience, story angle, recommended format, visual asset, opening hook, platform adaptations, caption copy, keywords, hashtag bundle, CTA, suggested posting window, and optional comment prompt. The document will also include paid-ad scripts and a final publishing checklist.
