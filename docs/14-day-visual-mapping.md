## Screenshot handling rules

The ten source screenshots should remain recognizable and unaltered in their product UI. Additional text should be limited to a concise social headline, a small highlighted label, or a numbered step. The 16:9 screenshots are the default source for LinkedIn, Facebook, and X. The 9:16 versions are preferred for TikTok and Instagram Reels. For carousel posts, retain a safe margin around UI text and never crop away the primary action being discussed.

## Visual consistency

Every post should use the Workshop Noir palette: charcoal surfaces, white interface text, steel-gray secondary text, and ember-orange emphasis. Use one dominant screenshot per post except on Days 11–14, where a comparison or sequence is the point. Screenshots should be paired with a short overlay headline rather than a dense poster-style block of copy.
