## Personalization checklist

Before sending, replace every bracketed field and mention one specific article, event, discussion, or community program. Choose only one guide that directly matches the recipient’s audience. Keep the request editorial and optional: the goal is to offer a useful resource, not to demand a link.

A relevant target may be a Nigerian developer community, university innovation hub, AI meetup, startup publication, marketing association, creator newsletter, or professional learning group. Prefer a genuine relationship, contribution, or resource exchange over bulk outreach.

## Responsible backlink guidance

The strongest links will come from pages where PromptForge is genuinely useful to the reader. Prioritize original educational contributions, event recaps, community resource pages, founder interviews, university or accelerator learning hubs, and relevant Nigerian technology discussions. Use descriptive anchor text naturally, and link to the most relevant guide rather than always linking to the homepage.

Do not buy large batches of links, submit the same message to many unrelated sites, create fake profiles, or publish invented testimonials and case studies. Track the recipient, date, page context, response, and live URL in a simple outreach sheet. A small number of relevant editorial mentions is more valuable for trust and long-term discoverability than a large number of low-quality placements.
