# PromptForge 30-Second Mobile Social Teaser

## Format

This teaser is **1080×1920 (9:16)** and 30 seconds long. It begins with an animated text-only **PROMPTFORGE** wordmark: `PROMPT` appears in white, then `FORGE` resolves in ember orange with a brief glowing underline. The opening must stay clear for three seconds before the product demonstration begins.
